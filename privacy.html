Privacy Policy for Pitch Caller Elite

Last Updated: September 2026

At Pitch Caller Elite, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Our application is built for tactical dugout use where security and privacy are paramount. This policy explains our data practices.

1. Zero-Data Collection Policy

Pitch Caller Elite is a local utility app. We do not collect, harvest, or share any personal information. Any data you enter into the app—including pitcher names, customizable buttons, secret terminology, and your analytics history—is stored exclusively on your physical device. We have no access to this data and do not use external servers.

2. Microphone and Bluetooth Privacy

The app requires specific permissions to function in the battery environment:

  • Microphone Access: Used only when you utilize the Live Coaching (Mic) feature. Your voice is processed locally to be relayed to the connected Bluetooth device. We never save, record, or transmit your voice data to the cloud.
  • Bluetooth: Used solely to broadcast audio signs and coaching commands to the catcher's earpiece or headset.

3. Local Storage and Data Control

All configurations and scouting reports are saved using standard system local storage.

  • You can clear all session data or entire pitcher profiles at any time within the app settings.
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all locally stored data.

4. Third-Party Services

Pitch Caller Elite does not utilize third-party analytics (like Google Analytics), tracking, or advertising networks. We do not sell or share user data because we simply do not have it.

5. Contact for Data Deletion

As we do not store data on our own servers, we do not possess any data to delete upon request. However, for support or questions regarding your local data safety, please contact: pitchcaller.support@gmail.com

Pro-Grade Pitching Analytics

Data-driven decisions shouldn't wait until after the game. Pitch Caller Elite provides real-time diagnostic tools to help coaches identify fatigue, mechanical slips, and opponent weaknesses as they happen.

🔥 Precision Heatmaps

Visualize "Pitch Density" and "Pitch Success" side-by-side. Identify exactly where your pitcher is dominating and where they are missing their spots.

📉 Fatigue Tracking

Monitor inning-by-inning performance. Visual bar charts track accuracy percentage and pitch load to help you make the right call for the bullpen.

🎯 Advanced Grid Matrix

Go beyond the 3x3 zone. Track balls, "chase" pitches, and diagnostic outcomes with our high-fidelity matrix system.

📊 High-Fidelity PDF Reports

Export professional scouting reports with zone-by-zone pitch counts and coaching notes. Share instantly via AirDrop, Text, or Email.

✏️ Total Customization

🎧 Stealth Audio

Broadcast signs instantly to any Bluetooth earpiece. Adjustable pitch and speed ensure crystal clear delivery.

🤝 Smart Shake Off

Privacy & Data Safety

Last Updated: September 2026

Pitch Caller is built for elite tactical security. We follow a strict Zero-Data Collection policy to ensure your competitive edge stays in your dugout.

  • No Cloud Storage: All pitcher profiles, history, and analytics are stored 100% locally on your device.
  • Permissions: Microphone access is used solely for the Live Coaching feature and Bluetooth for signaling. No data is ever shared.
  • Anonymous Use: No accounts, sign-ups, or tracking. Ready to call games instantly.

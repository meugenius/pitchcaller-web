Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023

Pitch Caller is committed to your privacy. Because our app is designed for elite security on the field, we follow a strict Zero-Data Collection policy:

  • No Data Collection: Pitch Caller does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, location data, or pitcher performance data to external servers.
  • Local Storage Only: All pitcher profiles, history logs, and analytics are stored 100% locally on your device.
  • No Accounts Required: You do not need to create an account or provide an email to use the app.
  • Offline Ready: The app functions entirely without an internet connection to ensure your data stays in your dugout.
